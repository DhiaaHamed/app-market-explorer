READ THE FINE PRINT

A useful lens.
Not the whole market.

This dashboard uses the historical app dataset supplied with a DataCamp learning project. The collection date and sampling method are not established. It cannot measure current market share, demand or business opportunity.

Average ratings give each rated app equal weight. Median price includes paid apps only. Install bands are lower bounds, not exact downloads. Review counts do not identify unique people. Missing ratings stay missing.

The interactive interface and filtering logic are a new portfolio extension built with AI assistance. The data originates from the earlier Google Play analysis; source-data rights remain with their owners. No live API, tracking or account is required.
